# Weather and Force Majeure

Owner: Legal

## Declared Events
Operations declares a force majeure event for named storms, government closures of major highways, and natural disasters affecting a service region.

## Effect on SLA
Orders whose delivery window overlaps a declared event are excluded from SLA credits. The exclusion ends 48 hours after the event is lifted.

## Customer Communication
Customers in affected regions receive a proactive notice from Customer Service.
