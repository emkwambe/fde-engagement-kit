# Ticket Priority Levels

Owner: Customer Service

| Priority | Use when | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Platinum order at risk or lost shipment | 1 hour |
| P2 | Gold order late, or credit above $1,000 | 4 business hours |
| P3 | Standard or Silver credit requests | 1 business day |
| P4 | Information requests | 2 business days |
