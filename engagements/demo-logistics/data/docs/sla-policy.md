# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

Owner: Operations · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Applies to all contracts signed on or after 2024-01-01.

## Delivery Commitments
Each customer tier has a committed delivery window, measured from order creation to proof of delivery:

| Tier | Committed delivery window |
|---|---|
| Standard | 5 calendar days |
| Silver | 4 calendar days |
| Gold | 3 calendar days |
| Platinum | 2 calendar days |

An order is late when proof of delivery is after the promised timestamp. Each started 24-hour period after the promised time counts as one day late.

## Credits
When an order is late, the customer is entitled to a credit on the order value:

| Tier | Credit per day late | Maximum credit |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 2% | 10% |
| Silver | 3% | 15% |
| Gold | 5% | 25% |
| Platinum | 7% | 35% |

Example: a Gold order worth $4,000 delivered 3 days late earns 15% ($600).

## Exclusions
No credit is owed when the delay is caused by a declared force majeure event (see weather-and-force-majeure.md), an incorrect address supplied by the customer, or the customer refusing delivery.

## Claim Window
Customers must request a credit within 30 days of delivery. Credits are applied to the next invoice, not paid as cash refunds (see refunds-vs-credits.md).
