# Customer Tiers

Owner: Sales Operations

## Tier Definitions
- **Standard:** default tier for new accounts.
- **Silver:** customers with more than $50,000 annual shipping spend.
- **Gold:** customers with more than $250,000 annual spend or a signed priority agreement.
- **Platinum:** strategic accounts approved by the VP of Operations.

## Tier Reviews
Tiers are reviewed every January. A customer keeps its tier until the next review even if spend drops mid-year.

## Where to Find the Tier
The tier is stored on the customer record in the reporting database (customers.tier).
