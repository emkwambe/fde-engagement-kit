# Legacy Contracts (Signed Before 2024)

Owner: Account Management · Last reviewed: 2025-06-15

## Scope
Some customers still operate under contracts signed before 2024-01-01. These contracts are flagged with "LEGACY" in the CRM account notes.

## Legacy Credit Terms
Legacy Gold contracts receive a credit of 4% per day late, capped at 20%. All other legacy tiers follow the current SLA policy.

## Conflicts
If the legacy contract and the current SLA policy disagree, the signed contract wins. Agents must check the account notes before quoting a credit. When the contract status is unknown, quote the current policy and flag the case for Account Management review.
