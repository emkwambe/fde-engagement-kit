# Lost Shipments

Owner: Claims

## When Is a Shipment Lost?
A shipment is considered lost when there has been no carrier scan for 7 calendar days, or the carrier confirms loss.

## Actions
1. Open a trace request with the carrier.
2. Notify the customer within 1 business day.
3. If not found within 10 days, reship at no cost or credit the full order value.
