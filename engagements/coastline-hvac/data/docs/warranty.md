# Parts and Labor Warranty

Owner: Service Contracts.

## Parts Warranty
Parts installed by Coastline are covered from the installation date:
- Basic plan: 1 year.
- Plus and Premium plans: 5 years.
- Compressors on Premium plans: 10 years.

## Labor Warranty
Labor on any repair is covered for 90 days. A repeat visit for the same fault within 90 days is free of charge.

## Not Covered
Damage from flooding, power surges, or equipment modified by another contractor.
