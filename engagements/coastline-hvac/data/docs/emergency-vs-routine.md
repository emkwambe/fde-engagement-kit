# Emergency vs Routine Calls

Owner: Dispatch Operations.

## Emergency Calls
A call is an emergency when any of these is true:
- No heat and the outdoor temperature is below 40 F.
- No cooling and the outdoor temperature is above 90 F.
- Water is leaking from HVAC equipment into the building.
- A refrigerant leak is suspected.
- A gas smell is reported. The dispatcher first tells the customer to leave the building and call the gas utility.

## Routine Calls
Everything else is routine: maintenance visits, noisy equipment, thermostat questions, reduced performance.
