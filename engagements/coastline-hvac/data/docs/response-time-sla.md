# Response-Time SLA

Owner: Dispatch Operations. Applies to every active service contract.

## Response Times
The response time runs from the moment a work order is opened until a technician responds on site.

| Plan | Emergency | Routine |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 24 hours | 72 hours |
| Plus | 8 hours | 48 hours |
| Premium | 4 hours | 24 hours |

## Credits
When the response time is missed, the customer receives a credit for each started 4 hours beyond the response time,
as a percentage of the monthly plan fee:

| Plan | Monthly fee | Credit per started 4 hours late | Maximum credit per work order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $49 | 5% | 25% |
| Plus | $89 | 10% | 40% |
| Premium | $149 | 15% | 50% |

Example: a Premium emergency answered 3 hours late is one started block: 15% of $149 = $22.35.
Credits are applied to the next monthly invoice; they are never paid out in cash.

## Exclusions
No credit is owed when the county declares a severe-weather emergency, when the technician could not get access to the
property, or when the work order was opened by mistake.

## Claim Window
Customers must claim a credit within 30 days of the missed response.
