# After-Hours Fees

Owner: Billing.

## After-Hours Window
After hours means 6 pm to 7 am on weekdays, all day on weekends, and all day on company holidays.

## Fees
A dispatch that starts after hours carries a flat fee per visit, by plan:

| Plan | After-hours fee |
|---|---|
| Basic | $150 |
| Plus | $75 |
| Premium | $0 (included) |

The fee is never above $150. Routine work is not scheduled after hours unless the customer asks and accepts the fee.
